## About this collection

Alongside research and client work, I write teaching material: course notes, exercise sheets and popular-science articles. This page gathers four sets of it. Each set is available in full through the link under its heading.

## Lecture notes in mathematics for the Domi Institute

A course in mathematics written in French for the Domi Institute. Each lesson comes as three documents: the theory, a set of exercises, and fully worked corrections, so that students can study on their own and check every step. Two lessons are available:

- **Algèbre élémentaire I** (elementary algebra), with its exercises and corrections;
- **Équations II** (equations), with its exercises and corrections.

[Open the lecture notes (Google Drive)](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Uz3PRJfA5IgBa9RMbYnxo6FB1ayRrM57?usp=sharing)

## GRE maths worksheets

Six worksheets for the quantitative section of the GRE, one topic each:

- exponents and roots;
- algebra;
- inequalities and absolute values;
- fractions and decimals;
- percents;
- divisibility and primes.

[Open the GRE worksheets (Google Drive)](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1hchkc0vM3_8ZYB6dylreo8Dfa97A4oD5?usp=sharing)

## Maths worksheets for the J.J. Bootcamp

A series of 35 "mission" worksheets in school mathematics, written for the J.J. Bootcamp. Each mission is a short, self-contained sheet that practises one skill.

[Open the worksheets (Google Drive)](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1r4_8Akmj5j32gnl80Np5MxxqEA5wkpzL?usp=sharing)

## Astronomy section of Space Magazine

I wrote the astronomy section of Space Magazine for four monthly issues, from May to August. The folder contains the material for each issue, and the complete June and July issues as PDFs.
