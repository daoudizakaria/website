### 3 The texts

#### 1. Gamma Sagittae 

Located in the arrow-shaped constellation of Sagitta, Gamma Sagittae is a red giant starshining with a distinct orange hue. Around 260 light years away, it marks the shaft of thecelestial arrow and is easily visible without optical aid.

#### 2. Gamma Delphini

A beautiful double star in Delphinus, Gamma is best viewed through binoculars or a smalltelescope. The system consists of a yellow and an orange star, offering a pleasing contrastin color.

#### 3. Epsilon Delphini

(Deneb Dulfim)This blue-white giant is the brightest star in the small constellation of Delphinus. About358 light years from Earth, Epsilon Delphini marks the tip of the dolphin’s tail and iseasily seen with the naked eye.

#### 4. Zeta Aquilae

Part of the Aquila constellation, Zeta Aquilae is a binary star system around 83 light yearsaway. To the naked eye, it appears as a single star, but binoculars reveal a subtle colordifference between its components.

#### 5. Alpha Aquilae

 (Altair) this brilliant white star is the twelfth brightest in the night sky and forms one corner ofthe Summer Triangle. Altair lies just 17 light years away, making it one of the closestbright stars to Earth. It spins so rapidly that it bulges at its equator.
